## Taxonomy implications

The corpus supports the pitched taxonomy-by-guarantee-mechanism and adds texture:
- **Constrained architectures** — CMNN/Sartor/min-max/monotonic-dense + isotonic-embedding, KAN-monotonic.
- **Lattice** — deep lattice nets + lattice LDA/SVM descendants.
- **Certification / verification** — counterexample-trained, positivity certification, REGLO, MoST (verifiable).
- **Soft / penalty** — PenDer, ICEnet, sensitivity-direction learning, calibration nets.
- **Isotonic & classical** — monotone GBM (knowledge-intensive), isotonic trees.
- **Flows / injective** — thin here (4 hits); expand via targeted anchors (own `injective-monotonic-flows` line).
